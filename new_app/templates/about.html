Our story

Why we started

ByteCare began with a simple idea: when a computer breaks, you should understand what is wrong, what it will cost, and what happens next, without technical jargon.

We work on laptops, desktops and home or office networks. Every job begins with a proper check, and you hear the result in plain language before any repair starts.
