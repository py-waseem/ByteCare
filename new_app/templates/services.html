Questions

Common questions

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on the problem. We check the device first and tell you the price before we start. If you decline the repair, you pay nothing more.

How long does a repair take?

Simple jobs such as software problems are often done the same day. Parts that must be ordered take longer, and we tell you the time when we quote.

Will I lose my files?

We back up your files before any repair that could affect them, and we tell you first if something puts your data at risk.

Do you visit homes and offices?

Yes, for network setup and on-site support. Contact us with your location and the problem.

Not sure what you need?

Describe the problem and we will point you the right way.
